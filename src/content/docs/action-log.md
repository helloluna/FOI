# Action Log – WA Animal Welfare Transparency  
**Maintained by Hanyu Liu**  
_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

This is a structural record — not of what was achieved,  
but of what was attempted, requested, refused, and left undocumented.

Each entry is part of a wider cycle:  
→ Legal request → Institutional response or silence → Public memory trace.  
This log preserves that cycle,  
**not as progress — but as accountability.**

**How to read symbols:** `✓` disclosed or confirmed · `~` procedural step or pending · `∅` documents not found or assessed as nonexistent within the stated scope · `✖` access withheld or a request declined.

**Evidence note (November 2026):** The original chronology is retained where possible. A section 26 decision is not proof that the underlying activity never occurred; an exemption is not a finding of nonexistence. Dates in the new entries are the latest confirmed events in the records examined, not predictions of the current outcome. Newly received documents are identified by date and reference while their publication copies are checked for personal information and restrictions. See [Resources](/resources).

### 📝 FOI1 Summary

**What was asked:**  
Aggregate DI inspection counts, summaries of inspection outcomes, and internal evaluations, reports or audits of DI capacity, numbers, training and resource allocation, for the period 1 January 2022 to 5 April 2025.

**What the evidence establishes (updated after external review):**
- Individual inspections took place and related records were identified.
- The requested consolidated statistics and outcome summaries were not located and were considered likely never to have been created.
- DPIRD told the OIC that the particular evaluations, reports and audits sought had not been undertaken.
- A new statistics document was compiled from raw data for the applicant. The OIC treated it as a courtesy release, outside the formal FOI decision.

**Structural question — Result vacuum:** How are regulatory results consolidated and assessed? The documented absence concerns specified summary and evaluation records, not all inspection activity or records. The earlier interpretation of the internal review as a direct contradiction between “no records” and “records exist” is superseded by the OIC's distinction between aggregate and individual records.

**Status:** ER1 completed — F2025236, decision D0182026, 7 September 2026. The decision was varied to refusal under section 26 for the entire application. See [OIC published decisions (2026), D0182026](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/office-of-the-information-commissioner/commissioners-foi-act-decisions-2026-2030-office-of-the-information-commissioner), especially paragraphs 6–9, 13–21 and 22–25.

## 🔄 FOI Process Flow

**FOI application** → **Agency access decision** (release, edited release, exemption, section 26 refusal or deemed refusal) → **Internal review where available** → **External review by OIC** → **Final access decision**.

A section 26 review addresses the requested existing documents and the agency's searches. The broader administration of a regulatory function is a different question.

**Other oversight pathways are separate, not automatic appeals from the OIC:**

- Ombudsman: individual administrative complaints subject to statutory requirements and discretion; systemic investigations may be considered independently.
- Auditor General: audit topics selected through the Auditor General's own process.
- Parliament and its committees: submissions may inform parliamentary scrutiny, but do not themselves initiate an inquiry.

**Public archive:** records what each pathway can establish and what remains unanswered.

## 📖 Glossary of Key Terms

- **FOI (Freedom of Information)** – Legal right to request government documents.  
- **Section 26** – Access refusal where reasonable searches have been undertaken and the agency is satisfied the requested document cannot be found or does not exist.  
- **IR (Internal Review)** – Second look by the same agency when you challenge a decision.  
- **ER (External Review)** – Independent review of an agency decision under the FOI Act. In a section 26 matter, the inquiry concerns the requested documents and adequacy of searches.  
- **OIC (Office of the Information Commissioner)** – Handles external review; a backlog is acknowledged and a queue management policy is now published.  
- **Ombudsman** – Investigates administrative action within its jurisdiction and discretion; not an automatic further appeal from an FOI review.  
- **OAG (Office of the Auditor General)** – Audits government operations; distinct from FOI external review.  
- **DPIRD (Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development)** – WA government department responsible for animal welfare enforcement.  
- **AWAC (Animal Welfare Advisory Committee)** – Non-statutory advisory committee; its annual reports for 2022 and 2023 were disclosed in FOI5.
- **Vacuum** – An analytical term for a documented gap in accessible records, specified management activities or substantive oversight; not proof that every related activity is absent.  
- **Deadlock** – An observed situation in which available pathways do not produce the substantive review sought; not a finding that all oversight mechanisms are unavailable.
